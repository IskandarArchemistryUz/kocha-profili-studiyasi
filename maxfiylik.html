2. Your projects

Drawn projects are stored in the User’s browser (on the device) and are not sent to the server. When cloud saving is enabled, the project is stored on the server — you will be notified separately.

3. Where and how long data is stored

  1. Personal data is stored on a server located in the Republic of Uzbekistan.
  2. Account data is kept until the account is deleted; payment records — for the period required by tax and accounting law. Sign-in codes expire in 10 minutes, sessions in 60 days.
  3. Backups are kept for 14 days.

4. Who receives data

Data is never sold. It is shared only to the extent necessary for the Service to work:

  • Google — if you choose to sign in with Google;
  • email service (Zoho Mail) — to send sign-in codes;
  • Payme, Click, Multicard — for payments;
  • map sources (OpenStreetMap, OpenFreeMap, Esri, AWS terrain tiles) — when maps load, your browser contacts them directly and they see your IP address;
  • state authorities — only in cases established by law.

5. Cookies and browser storage

  • ks_sess — sign-in session (60 days, read only by the server).
  • Browser storage — language, theme, projects and settings.
  • Analytics: GoatCounter, cookieless and without personal data — only the event name (e.g. which step was opened). With “Do Not Track” enabled nothing is sent.

6. Your rights

You have the right to know about your data, to correct and delete it, and to withdraw your consent to its processing. Send requests to info@archemistry.uz; we respond within 10 business days. If the account is deleted, it can no longer be accessed and unused credits are lost (except for the refund terms in section 6 of the offer).

7. Consent

By creating an account, the User consents to the processing of the data listed in this policy for the stated purposes.
